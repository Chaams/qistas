# Qistas — explainer video, three storylines

The one thing: **before a PR goes to production, Qistas weighs the Arabic chatbot and stops it if it gets worse.**
What the viewer remembers: "If my Arabic bot answers in the wrong dialect, makes things up or leaks data, the PR does not get through."

## A. Friday, 5:58 pm (recommended: a real situation the viewer knows, one example followed to the end)
- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day + an object-to-object chain (the chat bubble becomes a test case, which becomes a weight on the balance, which becomes the PR comment).
- **What you see:** a customer of a (fictional) Gulf bank asks about fees in dialect; a PR has just modified the bot's prompt; the bot answers in Egyptian Arabic, invents a figure and leaks another customer's IBAN. Qistas weighs the PR, the balance tips, the PR is blocked; the fix brings it back into balance.
- **First 2 s:** a phone, `الجمعة 17:58`, the bubble «ابي اعرف رسوم التحويل الدولي»; the bot's reply appears with an IBAN `SA03…` circled in red.
- **Beats:** hook (the leak) → why the usual tools miss it (dialect, arabizi, local IDs) → Qistas named → the PR goes through the 4 steps → the balance tips, ❌ comment → fix → ✅, back in balance → "Qistas weighs every PR" → github.com/Chaams/qistas.
- **Look:** blue-tinted paper, indigo ink, brass for the balance only; IBM Plex Sans / Plex Arabic; the real PR comment.
- **Sound:** calm, light music, a soft "clack" when a weight lands, a low note on ❌, a bright note on ✅.

## B. The balance
- **Device / form:** the object is the explanation + one continuous take around a large brass balance.
- **What you see:** each test case drops onto a pan as a weight labelled with its metric (dialect, fidelity, PII, injection); the "baseline" pan against the "PR" pan; the camera goes into a weight to show the judge giving its score.
- **First 2 s:** an empty balance, perfectly level; a first weight «golfe · 1.00» drops with a click.
- **Beats:** balance in equilibrium → the weights accumulate → one weight cracks (leak) → the balance tips → zoom on the cause → the PR is blocked → back in balance.
- **Look:** a single studio, matte metal, brass and ink; Arabic labels engraved on the weights.
- **Sound:** a low, steady pulse, metallic impacts, a silence at the moment it tips.

## C. ابي · عايز · أريد (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** type carries the argument + cuts on the beat.
- **What you see:** giant Arabic words morphing from one dialect to another (ابي → عايز → نحب → أريد), arabizi digits 3, 7, 9 turning into ع ح ق, an IBAN leaking letter by letter and then struck through; everything converges onto the word القسطاس.
- **First 2 s:** «ابي» full screen, which turns into «عايز» on the first beat.
- **Beats:** dialects → arabizi → leak → "a bot can get all of this wrong" → Qistas → 4 words: weigh, compare, block, fix → the name.
- **Look:** large colour fields (ink, brass, paper), Plex Arabic in very heavy weights.
- **Sound:** a rhythmic track with a clear beat, each cut on the beat.

**Assumptions:** 45 to 60 s, 16:9 (1920×1080), no voiceover, free royalty-free music with sound effects, on-screen text in French and Arabic. No paid elements.
